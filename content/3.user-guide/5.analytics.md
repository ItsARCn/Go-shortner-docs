# Analytics

GO Shortener provides comprehensive, aggregated click analytics for every link owned by a registered user, designed from the ground up with a **strict privacy-first architecture**.

---

## The Privacy-First Guarantee

Unlike many traditional tracking services that build invasive profiles or store visitor IP addresses, GO operates under strict privacy principles:

::important
**Zero Raw IP Storage**: Visitor IP addresses are immediately hashed using **SHA-256 with an identity salt** before click records or abuse counters are processed. The plain IP address is never stored in the database or written to disk logs.
::

### Privacy Protections:
- **No Third-Party Trackers**: Telemetry is processed entirely within your self-hosted Go binary; no third-party marketing tags, cookies, or external beacon scripts are used.
- **Aggregated Dimensions**: Data is grouped into broad categories (e.g., Browser family, Device category) rather than identifiable digital fingerprints.
- **GDPR & CCPA Friendly**: Because raw personal identifiers are not stored, compliance overhead is minimized.

---

## Available Metrics & Visualizations

When you click **Analytics** on any link in your dashboard, GO displays:

### 1. Volume & Trendlines
- **Total Lifetime Clicks**: The aggregate number of successful HTTP 302 redirects.
- **Temporal Trends**: Hourly clicks today, daily volume over the past 7 days, and monthly volume over the past 30 days.

### 2. Device Breakdown
Classifies incoming HTTP User-Agent headers into clean categories:
- **Desktop** (Windows, macOS, Linux)
- **Mobile** (iOS, Android smartphones)
- **Tablet** (iPads, Android tablets)

### 3. Browser Distribution
Identifies major browser engines:
- Google Chrome & Chromium variants
- Apple Safari
- Mozilla Firefox
- Microsoft Edge
- Other / Headless clients

### 4. Operating System Distribution
Groups visitor platforms:
- Windows
- macOS
- Linux
- Android
- iOS

### 5. Top Referring Domains
Parses the incoming `Referer` HTTP header to display the top websites and platforms directing traffic to your shortened link (e.g., `twitter.com`, `reddit.com`, `news.ycombinator.com`, or direct visits).
