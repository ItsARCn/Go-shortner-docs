# User Dashboard

The **User Dashboard** (`https://go.arcn.online/dashboard`) provides registered users with a unified command center for inspecting link metrics, tracking quota usage, and managing the lifecycle of shortened URLs.

---

## Top Metrics Overview

At the top of your dashboard, four primary summary cards display real-time usage statistics:

```text
+---------------------+---------------------+---------------------+---------------------+
| Links Used          | Total Clicks        | Active Links        | Expired Links       |
| 37 / 100            | 2,841               | 31                  | 6                   |
| Resets in 14 days   | All-time traffic    | Currently routed    | Available to renew  |
+---------------------+---------------------+---------------------+---------------------+
```

- **Links Used**: Shows how many links you have created against your 100 links/month quota, along with the days remaining until your monthly quota resets.
- **Total Clicks**: Cumulative redirect events across all your owned links.
- **Active Links**: Links currently receiving traffic and returning HTTP 302 redirects.
- **Expired Links**: Links that have passed their expiration date and are returning HTTP 410 Gone.

---

## Link Management Table

Below the metrics, the interactive links table provides comprehensive control over your catalog:

### Search & Filtering
- **Keyword Search**: Quickly locate short links by searching against the short code or any part of the destination URL.
- **Status Filter**: Toggle views between **All**, **Active**, and **Expired** links.
- **Pagination**: Navigate cleanly through large collections of links without performance degradation.

### Available Row Actions
For any given link row, the actions menu allows you to:
- **Copy Link**: Copies the full public short URL to your clipboard.
- **Analytics**: Opens the detailed analytics drawer with charts, devices, browsers, and referrers.
- **Renew**: Appears on expired links to restore them with a new expiration duration.
- **Request Permanent**: Opens the permanent link application form.
- **Delete**: Soft-deletes the link, removing it from your dashboard and immediately disabling redirection.
